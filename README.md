
###  **Project Overview: Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis**

This project presents a comprehensive sales analysis of historical transactional data from **Bright Coffee Shop**. The objective is to generate actionable business insights to support a newly appointed CEO in improving revenue performance, identifying growth opportunities, and optimizing product strategy.

The analysis explores revenue trends, customer behavior, and product performance to enable data-driven decision-making.
